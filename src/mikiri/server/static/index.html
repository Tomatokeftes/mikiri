Mikiri badminton movement, footwork and technique from video

Load a clip to begin

Open a clip and run tracking on the player: angles, events, an assessment and coaching follow. Calibrate the court, four clicks on its corners, for footwork in metres and rallies; track the player at the other end as well for serves, outcomes and the score.

For technique, film side-on, 4 to 5 m back, camera at hip to chest height, 120 fps or more. For the match figures, film from a raised camera behind one end. Mikiri measures in the image plane and on the court plane: it is a single-camera tool and says so where that limits it.

You can also drop a video here; it is copied into the Mikiri folder first.

                Court calibration

                Maps the floor in the video to a top-down court in metres. Choose which court points you can see, then click them on the video in the order given. Any frame works; the camera must not move during the clip.

                The posts are 1.55 m of known height standing at known court points, which is enough to work out where the camera is. That removes a systematic error in the far court -- most of a metre from a low tripod -- and lets heights be reported in metres. Two extra clicks; skip it and everything else still works.
